What's in the name?

Sakshi means someone who sees, understands, and remains aware.

For me, it means seeing beyond the surface, understanding how things work, and noticing opportunities for improvement. I do not know whether a name always reflects a person’s nature, but mine aligns closely with the way I think and work: observe carefully, understand deeply, and work thoughtfully.

Inspiration

MS Dhoni is my idol because he exemplifies calm leadership, resilience, and the ability to make effective decisions under pressure. His humility despite immense success inspires me to stay focused, grounded, and continuously strive for excellence.
